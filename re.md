# Terms of Reference (ToR) for  
## Ticket Platform and Event Website Development

---

## 1. Background

The Royal Government of Bhutan and the Conscious Food Systems Alliance (CoFSA), in collaboration with UNDP, and with Bhutan Global Mindfulness & MICE (BGMM) as the event management partner, will host the first Global Conscious Food Systems Summit in Bhutan from August 31 to September 4, 2026.

This landmark global gathering aims to facilitate high-level dialogue, knowledge exchange, innovation, and partnership-building around advancing conscious, inclusive, and sustainable food systems. Key thematic areas of the Summit include food consciousness, agroecology, the role of spirituality and culture in supporting nature-friendly agriculture, and the mobilization of collective action, movement-building, and investments to accelerate systemic transformation.

The Summit will also serve as a global platform to advance and showcase Bhutan’s leadership in national food systems transformation, highlighting the country’s unique pathways grounded in Gross National Happiness, sustainability, and harmony with nature.

The event is expected to convene 350–500 national and global changemakers, including representatives from governments and public institutions, indigenous communities, farmers’ groups, youth leaders, civil society, private sector actors, development partners, and academia. The Summit will prioritize gender balance, inclusion of persons with disabilities, and meaningful participation of underrepresented voices.

As part of the summit, a dedicated event website and ticketing platform will be developed to provide attendees with information about the event, facilitate online registration, and enable secure payment processing. The platform should align with the GCFSS branding and theme and provide an intuitive, professional, and seamless user experience for participants.

---

## 2. Objectives

To design, develop, and maintain a customized event website and ticketing platform that:

- Provides comprehensive information about the summit, speakers, agenda, useful information and activities.
- Supports a registration process, including a menu with options to be selected by the participants (field visits, different price tiers, other add=ons) and related database
- Implement a user-friendly admin panel for content management
- Provide responsive design across all devices
- Enables secure and seamless online registration
- Offers post-launch technical support until the end of the event.
- Collects and manages attendee data for reporting and analysis.
- SSL/TLS connections: Every connection to the site should be encrypted to protect users from man-in-the-middle attacks.
- Traffic: All traffic will be redirected using HTTPS protocol instead of HTTP.

## 3. Scope of Work

### 3.1. Event Website Design Adaptation

The contractor shall develop a professional, responsive website with the following features:

- **Custom Design:** Fully aligned with GCFSS branding, colors, and style guide.
- **Content Management System (CMS):** Ability to update event date, event name, countdown timer, speakers info, CTA for EoI, Events Plan, official sponsors lists, blogs posts and FAQs in real-time.
- **Analytics Integration:** Tools for tracking website traffic.
- **Mobile Compatibility:** Fully responsive design for access on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

#### 3.2.2. Online Registration and Payment Interface

- User registration and profiles.
- **User-Friendly Registration:** Streamlined process for registration including a menu with options to be selected by the participants (field visits, different price tiers, other add-ons).
- Following the registration, CoFSA and BGMM shall be able to review applications through an admin interface and approve, reject, or waitlist applicants. The system shall send automated notifications to applicants about their application status.
- **Attendee Management:** Tools to track registrations, generate reports, and manage attendee data.
- **Email Confirmation:** Automated confirmation emails upon successful registration (to confirm registration has been received and not yet approved) and payment. (with other mailing notes provided by BGMM)
- **Payment gateway and interface:** , and only approved applicants shall be sent an email with the ticketing system url for ticket purchase payment. A system will be in place for each participant to get the URL for payment based on the selected price and menu.
- **Package-Based Ticketing and Pricing System:** The platform shall support the creation and sale of participation packages during registration, including different pricing tiers.
- **Payment Processing:** Secure, reliable, and internationally compatible payment gateway(s), with currency conversion if required.
- Fortnightly submission of information to BGMM on the status of the ticket sales and BGMM to further share with the core team for tracking and interventions if required.
- **Data Access:** BGMM to have full access to registration information for administrative and reporting purposes.
- **Threat Preventions:** Secure the site from Malware, Viruses, phishing, Cross-Site Scripting, SQL injection and Ransome.
